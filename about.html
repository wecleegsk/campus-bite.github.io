Welcome to Campus Bite

Campus Bite is a food-ordering platform created for students at Tshwane University of Technology. It allows students to order meals quickly and conveniently from campus food vendors.

Our Mission

To make ordering food on campus simple, affordable, and convenient while supporting local campus food vendors.

Our Vision

To become the preferred food-ordering platform for students by providing reliable service, quality meals, and a great user experience.

Why Choose Campus Bite?

- Quick ordering process
- Affordable prices
- Convenient delivery
- Freshly prepared meals
- Easy-to-use website

Who We Serve?

Campus Bite is designed for:
- TUT students
- Campus staff
- Campus food vendors

How It Works

1. Browse the menu.
2. Select your meal.
3. Choose extras and a drink.
4. Select collection or delivery.
5. Submit your order.
6. Receive your meal.

Our Goal

Our goal is to reduce waiting times at campus cafeterias while giving students a simple and enjoyable way to order food.
