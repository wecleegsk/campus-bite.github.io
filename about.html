Welcome to Campus Bite

Welcome to Campus Bite, your one-stop student platform at Tshwane University of Technology (TUT). Campus Bite was created to make student life easier by providing affordable meals, convenient delivery and collection services, essential study items, and academic support. Our aim is to save students time while offering quality products and services in one place.

Our Mission

To provide TUT students with convenient, affordable, and reliable access to food, student essentials, and academic support, while creating opportunities for campus vendors and tutors to grow their businesses.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the leading student convenience platform at TUT by making everyday campus life simpler through innovative, accessible, and student-focused services.

Why Choose Campus Bite?

  • Quick ordering process
  • Affordable meals and student friendly prices
  • Convenient delivery
  • Freshly prepared meals
  • Easy-to-use website
  • Student essentials such as calculators and stationery
  • One-on-one tutoring sessions with experienced tutors
  • Reliable service designed specifically for TUT students

Who We Serve?

  • TUT students
  • Campus staff
  • Campus food vendors

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a single platform where TUT students can easily access food, study essentials, and academic support. We aim to improve student convenience, reduce waiting times, support campus entrepreneurs, and enhance the overall campus experience.
